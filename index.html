Ascent

Change one letter at a time and climb from the start word to the summit. A word ladder for iPhone and iPad, with a new daily climb for everyone.

How do I play?

Your word sits at the bottom of the ladder and the summit word at the top. Tap the letter you want to change, then tap its new letter on the keyboard. Every step must be a real word, and you change exactly one letter each time. Reach the summit to finish. The first level walks you through it.

Undo takes back the top rung. If you've wandered somewhere the summit can't be reached from, Ascent tells you, and Step back returns you to the last rung that still leads home.

What is par, and how do stars work?

Par is the fewest moves a climb can possibly be solved in — every puzzle is checked so that par is the true minimum and can be reached with everyday words. Finish at par for three stars, within two moves of par for two, and any finish earns one. The dashed rungs on your ladder show how many moves are left before par.

Why wasn't my word accepted?

Ascent's dictionary holds about 10,500 words of three to five letters, drawn from SCOWL and 12dicts, two long-standing open English word lists. It accepts American and British spellings. It leaves out very obscure words, abbreviations, proper names, and offensive words of every kind. If a word you know is real was refused, please email it — the list is reviewed with each update.

Some ordinary words are accepted when you type them but are never used in a puzzle or offered as a hint, so the game never hands anyone an awkward word.

How do hints work?

A hint plays the next word on a shortest route to the summit from wherever you are, even after a wrong turn. If the only way up runs back down your own ladder, the hint takes you back to that rung. You start with three hints and get one more each day you open the game, saving up to five. When you run out you can watch a short video for one, or get Ascent Plus for unlimited hints. Using hints never costs you stars.

The daily climb and streaks

Everyone gets the same ladder each day. A new one arrives at midnight where you are. Solve it to extend your streak; a streak survives until the end of the day after your last solve, so yesterday's streak is still alive until you've had today to play. Only your first solve of each day counts. Share your result from the result screen: the grid shows how close each rung came to the summit, never the words themselves.

The archive of past climbs

Every daily climb since #1 is kept in the archive, and a new one joins it at midnight. The most recent one, yesterday's, is free for everyone, so a missed day can always be caught up. Ascent Plus opens every other day in the archive. Archive climbs are saved part-way like any other, and they never change your streak: a streak belongs to each day's own climb, solved on its day.

Endless mode

Endless builds a fresh ladder on the spot at the gradient you choose: Gentle (three or four letters, three or four moves), Steady (four or five letters, four to six moves) or Steep (five letters, six to eight moves). Endless climbs aren't saved part-way; everything else is.

Widgets, controls and Siri

Add widgets from your Home Screen: touch and hold an empty area, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Ascent. There are ten, in every size from the Lock Screen to the iPad's extra large: Daily Climb, Streak, Next Climb (the time left today), Yesterday's Climb, Next Level, Hints and Endless for everyone, and Climb Calendar, Level Packs and Stats with Ascent Plus. To choose the difficulty an Endless widget starts, touch and hold it and tap Edit Widget. Widgets update at midnight on their own and whenever you play.

On iOS 18 and later, Ascent's controls can go in Control Centre, on the Lock Screen or on the Action button, to open today's climb, Endless or the archive in one press. Siri knows Ascent too: try “What's today's Ascent climb?” or “What's my Ascent streak?”.

The daily reminder

Turn it on in Settings > Reminder and choose a time. The notification names that day's ladder, and it's skipped on days you've already solved. If iOS has notifications for Ascent turned off, turn them on in the Settings app under Notifications > Ascent.

Why are there ads, and when do they appear?

Ads keep Ascent free for everyone. They are shown with care: never during a climb, never after the first level, not at all during your first five solves, and after that at most once every three solves with at least a couple of minutes in between. Videos for hints are only ever shown when you choose one. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.

Ascent Plus and its plans

Ascent Plus opens every past climb in the archive, removes every ad, makes hints unlimited, and adds the Climb Calendar, Level Packs and Stats widgets. All 200 levels, the daily climb and Endless stay free for everyone either way. There are three ways to have it, all with the same features:

  • Yearly, $19.99 a year in the US, with a one-week free trial for new subscribers;
  • Monthly, $2.99 a month in the US;
  • Lifetime, a single payment of $39.99 in the US, which never renews.

Other countries pay Apple's local price, and the app shows it before you buy. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. If you start the free trial and don't cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the yearly subscription begins and is charged when the trial ends.

Cancelling or changing a subscription

In Ascent, open Settings > Manage subscription. Or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Ascent Plus (Apple's guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple). Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge; Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Switching from monthly to yearly takes effect straight away, with a refund of the unused part of the month; switching from yearly to monthly takes effect at the next renewal.

Restoring on a new device, and refunds

Sign in with the same Apple Account and Plus comes back by itself; if it doesn't, open Settings > Restore purchases. Ascent Plus is not shared through Family Sharing. Payment, receipts and refunds are handled by Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Offer codes can be redeemed in Settings > Redeem offer code.

Changing your ad privacy choices

If you live somewhere that requires consent for ads (such as the EU, the UK or several US states), Ascent shows Google's consent form the first time ads come into play, and you can change your answers any time from Settings > Ad privacy choices. Whether apps may use your device's advertising identifier is controlled by iOS in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. More in the privacy policy.

Where is my progress kept?

On your device only. There's no account and no Ascent server. Deleting the app deletes your progress; Settings > Reset all progress erases it without deleting the app (Ascent Plus is kept, because Apple holds that record). A backup of your iPhone or iPad includes your progress.
